# Sister's Wedding Wish

Build a cozy, heartfelt, and highly personal single-page website dedicated to my sister on her wedding day. I am stuck in another country and can't attend, so this website is my digital love letter and memory scrapbook for her. It should not look like a professional, formal wedding site—it should feel deeply personal, warm, and sentimental.

Overall Style & Aesthetic:

Vibe: Nostalgic, intimate, and perfectly imperfect. Think of a physical scrapbook or a memory box.

Color Palette: Warm, comforting tones (creams, warm whites, soft paper textures, and maybe a subtle pastel).

Typography: Use a playful or messy handwritten-style font for the headings so it feels like I wrote it myself, paired with a soft, easy-to-read font for the longer text.

Page Structure & Features:

The Big Welcome: A hero section at the top featuring a single, joyful photo of us (or her). The main text should be in the handwritten font saying something like, "To my favorite sister on your wedding day," with a smaller note below saying, "Sending all my love from across the world."

My Letter to You: A section designed to look like a physical card or a piece of note paper. This is where I will put a long, emotional, and probably funny letter about how proud I am of her and how much I miss her today.

The Polaroid Gallery: A photo gallery designed to look like scattered Polaroids or printed photos resting on a table. Give the images white borders, slight tilts, and soft drop shadows to make them look real. When clicked, they should pop up larger.

Funny/Sweet Memories: A section with a layout for 3 or 4 short memories. Each should have a space for a small photo and a short caption underneath so I can remind her of our inside jokes.

The Sign-Off: A warm footer at the bottom that says, "Can't wait to hug you both. Have a slice of cake for me!"

Technical Details:

Make sure it looks great on a phone, as she will probably be looking at this on her phone during the wedding day.

Include fun, subtle animations like the "Polaroid" photos gently floating or fading in as she scrolls.
